Our Process

FROM ENQUIRY TO ENERGY FREEDOM

A proven four-step process that takes you from first contact to a fully commissioned off-grid system — with zero stress.

1

Site Analysis

We assess your location, sun hours, loads, and energy goals. Remote or on-site survey available worldwide.

2

Custom Design

Our engineers size every component — panels, battery, inverter — and produce a full electrical design with energy simulation.

3

Installation

Certified technicians handle mounting, wiring, and commissioning to the highest safety standards. Typically 2–5 days on site.

Our Story

ENGINEERING
ENERGY FREEDOM
SINCE 2010

Off Grid Systems was founded by a team of renewable energy engineers who believed that reliable, clean power shouldn't require proximity to a utility grid. We've spent over a decade designing and deploying autonomous solar PV systems across the planet's most remote and demanding environments.

From alpine chalets in the Swiss Alps to agricultural estates in the Australian outback, from Saharan telecom towers to Pacific island eco-resorts — we've proven that with the right engineering, solar energy can power anything, anywhere, anytime.
