Our Process

FROM ENQUIRY TO ENERGY FREEDOM

A proven four-step process that takes you from first contact to a fully commissioned off-grid system — with zero stress.

1

Site Analysis

We assess your location, sun hours, loads, and energy goals. Remote or on-site survey available worldwide.

2

Custom Design

Our engineers size every component — panels, battery, inverter — and produce a full electrical design with energy simulation.

3

Installation

Certified technicians handle mounting, wiring, and commissioning to the highest safety standards. Typically 2–5 days on site.

Monitor & Support

Remote monitoring, annual health checks, and 24/7 engineer support keep your system running at peak performance for decades.
