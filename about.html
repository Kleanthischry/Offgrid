Our Story

ENGINEERING ENERGY FREEDOM

Since 2010, we've deployed autonomous solar systems across the planet's most remote and demanding environments.

Off Grid Systems was founded by a team of renewable energy engineers who believed that reliable, clean power shouldn't require proximity to a utility grid. We've spent over a decade designing and deploying autonomous solar PV systems across the planet's most remote and demanding environments.

From alpine chalets in the Swiss Alps to agricultural estates in the Australian outback, from Saharan telecom towers to Pacific island eco-resorts — we've proven that with the right engineering, solar energy can power anything, anywhere, anytime.

Tier-1 Components Only

We specify only certified tier-1 panels, factory-authorised inverters, and proven lithium battery solutions from the world's leading manufacturers.

Full Turnkey Delivery

Site survey, energy analysis, custom system design, procurement, installation, commissioning, and long-term O&M — we own the whole process.

Global Reach, Local Expertise

Our certified partner network operates in 40+ countries, adapting every design to local climate, regulation, and grid interconnection standards.
